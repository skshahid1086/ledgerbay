Ledgerbay Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026 (Asia/Calcutta). App: Ledgerbay (in.ledgerbay.app), published by Sk Sahid.

Ledgerbay is an offline-first GST billing and accounting app for Indian businesses. Your books are meant to stay under your control.

Data stored on your device

Parties, items, invoices, purchases, payments, staff PINs you set, and the business profile you enter (name, address, GSTIN, PAN, bank and UPI details, logo, signature) are stored on your device. On Windows this is typically %APPDATA%\ledgerbay. On Linux it is typically ~/.config/ledgerbay. On Android it is the app’s local storage. Ledgerbay does not operate a cloud account for your books and does not sell or rent your data.

Password

An owner password (and optional staff PIN) is stored only on that device, as a salted hash, to unlock the app. It is not sent to a Ledgerbay server (there is none for your books).

Camera

The camera is used only when you tap Scan to read a product barcode, serial number or IMEI. Images are processed on the device for that scan and are not uploaded to a Ledgerbay server.

Network

You can use Ledgerbay without the internet. Network access is used for features you choose:

  • Optional device sync with a server you run (usually the Ledgerbay sync host on your own PC, on your local network, protected by a passcode you set). Sync traffic is sent to that address; it is not sent to a Ledgerbay-operated cloud.
  • Opening WhatsApp or a UPI payment link when you tap those actions. Those services then follow their own privacy policies.
  • If you install from a store, that store — not the Ledgerbay app itself — may check for app updates.

No advertising or analytics SDKs are bundled for tracking you across apps.

Backups

Export and Restore write or read a JSON file you choose. You decide where that file is kept. Ledgerbay can restore backups from earlier Ledgerbay releases and from BillSetu v19/v20 (the previous name).

Children

Ledgerbay is intended for business use by adults, not for children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions, email work.sahid@icloud.com. You can also open an issue on the GitHub Issues page.
