Ledgerbay Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026 (Asia/Calcutta). App: Ledgerbay (in.ledgerbay.app).

Ledgerbay is an offline-first GST billing app for Indian small businesses. We designed it so your books stay under your control.

Data we store

Parties, items, invoices, purchases, payments, and business profile details you enter are stored on your device (and, if you turn sync on, on your own sync server that you run). Ledgerbay does not operate a cloud account for your books and does not sell or rent your data.

Camera

The camera is used only when you tap Scan to read a product serial number or IMEI barcode. Images are processed on the device for that scan and are not uploaded to Ledgerbay servers (there are none for your books).

Network

Internet access is used for optional features you choose: syncing with a server you configure, opening WhatsApp or UPI links, and checking for updates if you install from a store. No advertising or analytics SDKs are bundled for tracking you across apps.

Backups

Export and Restore write or read a JSON file you choose. You decide where that file is kept. Ledgerbay can restore backups from earlier Ledgerbay releases and from BillSetu v19/v20 (the previous name).

Children

Ledgerbay is intended for business use by adults, not for children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions about this app listing, contact the developer email shown on the store listing page.
